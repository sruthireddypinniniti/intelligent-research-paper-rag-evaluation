# Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Artificial intelligence in healthcare includes machine learning methods that can support clinical decision-making, medical image analysis, patient risk prediction, and workflow automation.

Machine-learning systems can learn patterns from clinical or imaging data and provide decision-support signals for diagnostic workflows. Their performance depends on data quality, validation, and the clinical setting.

Healthcare AI research can be limited by dataset quality, distribution shift, bias, insufficient external validation, privacy requirements, and the difficulty of integrating models safely into clinical workflows.

Clinical deployment requires careful validation and attention to the setting in which a model will be used. A model that performs well on one dataset may not generalize to a different population or workflow.
